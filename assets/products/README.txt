YOUR PRODUCT PHOTOS GO HERE

Put your own photos in this folder. Example:
  assets/products/my-new-bag.jpg

Then in app.js, set that product's image field to:
  image: "assets/products/my-new-bag.jpg"

Use simple lowercase names with no spaces. JPG, PNG and WEBP work well.
The catalogue currently uses sample online photos until you replace each image path.
